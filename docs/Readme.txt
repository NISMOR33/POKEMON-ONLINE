Eternal Emerald Known Issues
This page lists currently known bugs in the game.
If new bugs are discovered during playthroughs, they will be added here.
Severity Guide
Red – Urgent issue
Orange – Minor issue
1. Soft Reset During Tournament Battles
If you soft reset during a Pokémon World Tournament battle instead of closing the game completely, experience will stop being gained in normal battles afterwards. Enemy trainers may also have the same number of Pokémon as the tournament format you selected.
Fix: Do not soft reset during Pokémon World Tournament battles. Close the game completely and reopen it to fix the issue.

2. Entering the Tournament After a Soft Reset
If you soft reset the game and then attempt to enter the Pokémon World Tournament, the game may crash before the tournament menu appears.
Fix: Do not soft reset before entering the Pokémon World Tournament. If you need to reset, close and reopen the game instead. The game saves before entering the tournament, so you will not lose progress.
 
3. Mt. Battle
During Mount Battle fights, if a Pokémon reaches a level where it would normally evolve, the evolution screen may briefly appear. However, the Pokémon will not actually evolve because experience is not truly awarded in Mount Battle.
This behavior is intentional, since Mount Battle battles do not grant real experience, but the evolution prompt may still appear due to how the battle system processes level checks.

4.Evolve Music Glitch
 When a Pokemon evolves and you quit the evolution the music will continue to play.
